# test

Test suites for `node --test`, run with `npm test` (in Docker, since the host has no Node). Each suite builds its own in-memory database and services with `createTestContext()`. The data-backed suites need a real artifact export, which is personal data and not in git: point `DUTCH_SEED` at it, or they are skipped.

## Files

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `seed.js` | Loads the seed export named by `DUTCH_SEED` (default `data/seed/dutch-export.json`) and gives the skip option when it is missing. |
| `database-test-helpers.js` | `createTestContext({ clock, isFsrsFuzzEnabled })`: an in-memory database with all migrations and the domain services built on it. The clock defaults to the system clock; fuzz is off in tests. |
| `domain-services.test.js` | Domain service behaviour: practice results, mistake logging, duplicate skipping, filters, errors, statistics and export. |
| `practice.test.js` | FSRS practice at service level with a fake clock: the migration (dry run and real, one `words` change after the commit), the batch quota and redistribution, grading once per session and its due dates, word errors, chapters and manual priorities, the Amsterdam day boundary including the DST day, the statistics, and the frequency import. |
| `practice-reset.test.js` | `resetPractice` with a fake clock: refused before the migration, a dry run writes nothing, a real reset leaves every word and phrase New with history kept and a batch of exactly 10 new words, skipped days afterwards never grow the list beyond the words started, today's extra words are cleared (the block is cleared too), a reset mid-session offers the words and phrases graded earlier in the same session again and lets them be graded once more (a third grade in that session is still refused), a dry run supersedes no review log and a real reset supersedes every one that counted (the logs stay as history), and the database refuses a second log for the same session and word while the first one still counts. |
| `practice-backlog.test.js` | The backlog gate, the intake block (a day left half done offers only its leftovers the next day, the same words; nothing done offers the same 10 again; finishing the leftovers opens a fresh 10 the same day; unstarted extra words carry over) and the extra new words for words at service level with a fake clock: overdue words hold back the New words (in any session), a word due earlier today or graded again does not, grading the last overdue word unlocks the day's new words in the same session, `addExtraNewWords` refused while a word is overdue or today's New words are not all introduced, 10 more split 7, 2, 1 after the regular 10, a second call refused until the first 10 extra words are graded and then 10 more split 7, 2, 1 (scaled quota 21, 6, 3), the extra resets on the next day, and the overdue and block fields of `practice_stats`. |
| `learning-deck.test.js` | The learning deck: the pure rules (2 right answers in a row for a clean word, +1 per again or hard up to 6, a lapse comes back with 3), and through grading with a fake clock: 9 missed and 1 right yesterday give 10 deck words and no new word today, repeats count, learned words free their slots for new words, and the deck rebuild (dry run, positions, a deck word due the day after its last answer). |
| `practice-intake-block.test.js` | The pinned intake blocks (§13.2) for words and phrases at service level with a fake clock: the leftovers are exactly the pinned words (words added later wait outside the block), a started word whose priority changes (`set_word_priority`, `set_current_chapter`) leaves the block as it is, grading a New word outside the block changes nothing, a deleted leftover counts as done and finishing the leftovers opens a fresh 10 the same day, a block opened today that is finished gives no new words, extra words append 10 ids split 7, 2, 1 and carry over unstarted, a block saved in the old format (no `item_ids`) reads as finished (an earlier day gives a fresh 10, today gives none), a block saved without `extra_count` gives 10 extra words when 10 are added, a fresh block pinned with 6 words and then 10 extra words counts 10, a word graded in a session and set back to New is held back in that session only, with no New words at all the extra words are not refused and nothing is saved, and the phrase equivalents (pinned leftovers, a phrase added later waits, a deleted leftover, a fresh 5 after the leftovers, a finished block stays until a later day). |
| `practice-struggling.test.js` | The struggling-words drill (§13.1) at service level: refused before the migration; the words whose latest answer was again or hard, most errors first then latest first, a later good drops a word out, a never-graded word is left out; and the drill writes nothing (review logs, word errors, cards and statuses are unchanged). |
| `phrase-practice.test.js` | FSRS practice of phrases at service level with a fake clock: refused before the migration, 5 New phrases a day oldest first, the allowance after grades, good > hard > again due dates, one grade per session, the due boundary at the start of tomorrow in Amsterdam, the batch cut to `maxReviews`, the phrase list's status and next due day, the phrase statistics, and the backlog gate for phrases (overdue phrases hold back New phrases until graded, the counts and `new_phrases_blocked`). |
| `practice-logic.test.js` | Pure practice modules without a database: calendar days and DST, FSRS scheduling and migration cards, chapter and priority rules, queue order, the frequency rank of a Dutch text (apostrophes, numbers, articles), new-word allocation (7, 2, 1 and the redistribution), the tier quota of a block (`calculateNewWordAllowance`: 10 gives 7, 2, 1, 20 gives 14, 4, 2, rounding down), the intake block (`resolveIntakeBlock`: leftovers are the pinned items still New, a deleted item counts as done, a block from an earlier day that is finished gives way to a fresh one, a block opened today stays), the saved form (`mapToStoredBlock`, `mapFromStoredBlock`: a block without `item_ids` reads as finished, one without `extra_count` has none) and the extra new words of a block opened today (`countExtraItemsToday`). |
| `import.test.js` | Import of the seed export through the artifact import service: counts, rule mapping, example splitting, idempotence and export round trip; and the refusal once FSRS is set up. |
| `http.test.js` | HTTP behaviour of the full application (`createApplication`): login, cookie and bearer authentication, rate limiting, REST contract, live-update stream, export download, and the REST practice routes (409 before the migration; batch, grades, repeat, stats and preview after it), the phrase practice routes (409 before the migration, batch, grade, repeat and an `answer` refused with 400), the phrase list's `status` and `next_due_on`, the `error_count` of a graded word, and `POST /api/practice/extra-new-words` (409 while today's New words are not all used up, including a second call before the first 10 extra words are graded; 400 for `count` 0 or an unknown field; 200 with the default count of 10 for an empty body), and `GET /api/practice/struggling-words` (409 before the migration, then the words to drill, read only). |
| `mcp.test.js` | MCP server over HTTP in the full application: instructions, tool list (including the practice tools), every tool's behaviour, `set_word_priority`'s compact word, the secret-path and bearer authentication, `grade_word` and `grade_phrase` round trips on separate databases (a repeat refused, `error_count` of the graded word, the phrase's status in `list_phrases`), the phrase practice tools in the tool list, and `add_extra_new_words` (listed, refused until the regular words are used up, then adds words in a round trip, and 10 when called with no arguments), and `get_struggling_words` (read-only, listed, a drill that changes no schedule or review log). |
| `environment-configuration.test.js` | The server refuses to start outside test mode when a required secret is missing; the configuration's defaults. |
| `ui-logic.test.js` | Pure browser helpers in `public/logic.js`: escaping, answer checking, `gradeTranslationAnswer` (the grade for a typed Dutch answer in FSRS practice; the article never changes the grade), `canOfferExtraNewWords` (when the «ще 10 нових слів?» offer shows), `revealTranslationAnswer` (the answer shown for «Не знаю»), `pluralizeDays`, `formatDaysAway`, `buildNextReviewLine` (the next-review line under a checked answer), `formatTimesCount` and `buildDeckLine` (the times left in the learning deck, or learned), `formatShortDate` (the table's «Наступне» date), `advanceDrillQueue`, `formatStrugglingDrillButton` and `buildStrugglingDrillEnd` (the struggling-words drill's queue, button text and end line), and rule-body rendering. |

## Conventions

Run the suite with:

```bash
docker run --rm -v /srv/dutch-project/app:/app -v /srv/data/dutch/seed:/seed:ro -e DUTCH_SEED=/seed/dutch-export.json -w /app node:24-bookworm-slim npm test
```
