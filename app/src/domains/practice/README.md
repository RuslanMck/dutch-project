# practice

Translation practice on an FSRS schedule: each word's card and due date, the daily allowance of new words and their priorities, the intake blocks that pin the new words offered together (a block stays open until every item in it is started or deleted, across days), the backlog gate that holds new words back while a word is overdue, the extra new words a learner can add to today's block, the frequency list that ranks words, the review log of graded answers, the struggling-words drill (words whose latest answer was again or hard, read only), the practice reset, the practice settings, and the same schedule for phrases (their own card, intake block of 5 new phrases with the same gate, and review log, without priorities or word errors). Pure scheduling, allocation and block rules have no I/O. Repositories hold the SQL only, services own the rules, and adapters expose the practice to REST and MCP. Dictionary CRUD and the old drill stay in `domains/words/`, and the frequency list file is read by the import script, not here.

## Files

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `practice-repository.js` | SQL for `practice_settings` (JSON values), the card, due, introduction and priority columns of `words` (read through `v_words`), the count of overdue words, the struggling words (each word whose latest review log is again or hard), and `review_logs`: session look-ups count only logs that still count (`superseded_at` NULL), and `markReviewLogsSuperseded` marks them at a practice reset. No business rules. |
| `phrase-practice-repository.js` | SQL for the card, due, introduction and status columns of `phrases`, and `phrase_review_logs` (session look-ups count only logs that still count; `markPhraseReviewLogsSuperseded` marks them at a practice reset). No business rules. |
| `frequency-repository.js` | SQL for `word_frequencies`: replaces or upserts the rank of each word form and looks up a rank by form, case-insensitively. No transactions; the service wraps them. |
| `practice-calendar.js` | Pure: calendar dates in the practice time zone (`Europe/Amsterdam`), start of a day and day arithmetic, DST-safe. Builds one `Intl` formatter per time zone and reuses it. |
| `fsrs-scheduling.js` | Pure wrapper around ts-fsrs: grades a card, serializes and parses cards, reads the scheduled interval, derives the learning status and the state name (from `CARD_STATE_VALUES`), builds the migration cards. |
| `priority-calculation.js` | Pure: parses the chapter from a section code, calculates a word's priority 1–3, compares queue order and computes a frequency rank for a Dutch text (apostrophes kept, numbers ignored, names in expressions unranked). |
| `new-word-allocation.js` | Pure: `calculateNewWordAllowance({ newPerDay, tierQuota, blockSize })` gives the tier quota of a block (each quota scaled by the block size and rounded down: 10 gives 7, 2, 1; 20 gives 14, 4, 2), and `allocateNewWords` chooses the New words of a block by priority quota, with unused slots going to P1, P2, P3. |
| `intake-block.js` | Pure: `resolveIntakeBlock` decides whether an intake block is open. A stored block (`{ openedOn, itemIds, extraCount }`) stays open while any of its pinned items is still New (a deleted or started item counts as done), and a block opened today stays once finished; otherwise `isFresh` is true, `extraCount` is 0 and the day's count is allocated. `openIntakeBlock` pins items, `mapToStoredBlock` and `mapFromStoredBlock` convert to and from the saved `{ opened_on, item_ids, extra_count }` (a block saved without `item_ids` reads as finished, one without `extra_count` has 0 extra words), and `countExtraItemsToday` returns the block's extra new words when it was opened today, 0 otherwise. |
| `migration-planning.js` | Pure: `planMigration` plans the one-time FSRS migration without writing (learning words spread over the next four days, most errors first; learned words due in 21 days; new words stay new) and `summarizeMigration` gives the counts the dry run and the real run both return. |
| `learning-deck.js` | Pure learning-deck rules (the deck is the current session's words): `applyAnswerToDeck` moves a word for one answer (a word enters on its first answer ever, or on a lapse, which it remembers as `isLapse`; good adds a right answer, again or hard add one required right answer up to 6 and keep the right answers given; the right answers reaching the required count means learned), `countTimesLeft`, `readDeckPosition` (from the `deck_*` columns), `describeDeckPosition` (the `deck` fields of the adapters) and `replayDeckAnswers` (for the rebuild). |
| `new-word-planning.js` | Factory `createNewWordPlanner({ practiceRepository })`: `planNewWords` plans the New words on offer (the backlog gate, the intake block from `intake-block.js` — its leftovers, or a fresh allocation of `new_per_day` words once none of the session's New words is left in the learning deck (lapses do not count), on any day — the words offered by priority and the remaining counts, and the extra words of today), `planExtraNewWordIds` picks the ids of extra words outside the block, and `assertExtraNewWordsAllowed` refuses extra new words while a word is overdue or the block is not finished. Reads only; writes nothing. |
| `practice-settings.js` | Service: reads the practice settings in camelCase (`readSettings`, including `phraseNewPerDay` and the intake blocks `newWordBlock` / `newPhraseBlock` as `{ openedOn, itemIds, extraCount }`, null when none is stored); `readActiveSettings` is the one guard that refuses practice before the FSRS migration. Saves the current chapter and the intake blocks, tells whether FSRS is active and records the migration time. |
| `priority-service.js` | Service: recalculates priorities (`recalculatePriorities` publishes; `recalculatePrioritiesInTransaction` is for a caller that already holds a transaction and publishes nothing), ranks words from the frequency list (`assignNewWordRanking` also runs inside the caller's transaction), sets manual priorities and the current chapter, previews priorities for a chapter, and imports the frequency list in one transaction. Publishes `words` changes. |
| `practice-service.js` | Service: builds the practice batch (reviews, and the New words of the intake block, held back while any word is overdue, reported as `counts.new_words_blocked`), adds extra new words to today's block (`addExtraNewWords({ count })`, refused while a word is overdue or the block is not finished), grades a word once per session (the first grade of a New word that opens no block pins the day's allocation as the block; a wrong grade is recorded through `wordService.recordPracticeError`; the graded word carries its `error_count`), lists the struggling words (`listStrugglingWords`, read only), runs the migration to FSRS (dry run or real; one transaction, one `words` change after the commit) and calculates the practice statistics, with the phrases' own numbers under `phrases`. Publishes `words` changes. |
| `practice-reset-service.js` | Service: `resetPractice({ isDryRun })` starts the practice over: every word and phrase with a card, due date or introduction goes back to New, both intake blocks are cleared and every review log that still counts is superseded, all in one transaction (the scheduled rows are read inside it); review logs and word errors are kept. Refused before the FSRS migration. Publishes `words` and `phrases` changes after a real reset. |
| `phrase-practice-service.js` | Service: the phrase batch (phrases due before tomorrow, most overdue first, plus the New phrases of the phrase intake block — 5 a day, oldest first, the pinned leftovers while a block is open, none while a phrase is overdue), grading a phrase once per session, and the phrase statistics. Gated by the same `readActiveSettings` guard as words. Publishes `phrases` changes. |
| `practice-tools.js` | MCP adapter: registers the thirteen practice tools (`get_practice_batch`, `grade_word`, `get_phrase_batch`, `grade_phrase`, `add_extra_new_words`, `set_current_chapter`, `set_word_priority`, `import_frequency_list`, `preview_priorities`, `migrate_to_fsrs`, `reset_practice`, `practice_stats`, `get_struggling_words`). `set_word_priority` returns the compact word of the word tools, with `priority_override`; `get_struggling_words` is read only. |
| `round-summary.js` | Pure: the round's words with answers, mistakes and difficulty, the hardest word, and the time spent without pauses. |
| `practice-routes.js` | REST adapter: `GET /api/practice/batch`, `POST /api/practice/grades`, `GET /api/practice/phrases/batch`, `POST /api/practice/phrases/grades`, `POST /api/practice/extra-new-words`, `GET /api/practice/stats`, `GET /api/practice/struggling-words` (read only), `GET /api/practice/round-summary` (read only) and `GET /api/practice/priorities/preview`. |

## Conventions

- Repositories and the frequency repository are created with `create…Repository({ database })` and prepare their statements once in the factory.
- Values are bound parameters. JSON columns (`fsrs_card`, `review_logs.card_before`, `card_after`, settings) are stored as text; the settings repository parses and stringifies them.
- Pure modules take `now` and the other values as parameters and never read the clock or the database.
- Every practice service reads its settings through `practiceSettings.readActiveSettings()`, so the refusal before the migration reads the same everywhere.
- The rules are in `project-quality-standards` and in `docs/FSRS_PRACTICE_ARCHITECTURE.md` (sections 1 to 4, 10.1 for the backlog gate, 11 for the reset, 12 and 13.2 for the pinned intake blocks, and 13.1 for the struggling words).
