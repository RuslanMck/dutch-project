# practice

Translation practice on an FSRS schedule: each word's card and due date, the daily allowance of new words and their priorities, the practice sets (a session's cards, at most 10: old ones first, new ones only in the room left), the frequency list that ranks words, the review log of graded answers, the struggling-words drill (words whose latest answer was again or hard, read only), the practice reset, the practice settings, and the same schedule for phrases (their own card, set of at most 10 and review log, without priorities or word errors). Pure scheduling, allocation and set rules have no I/O. Repositories hold the SQL only, services own the rules, and adapters expose the practice to REST and MCP. Dictionary CRUD and the old drill stay in `domains/words/`, and the frequency list file is read by the import script, not here.

## Files

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `practice-repository.js` | SQL for `practice_settings` (JSON values), the card, due, introduction and priority columns of `words` (read through `v_words`), the count of overdue words, the struggling words (each word whose latest review log is again or hard), and `review_logs`: session look-ups count only logs that still count (`superseded_at` NULL), and `markReviewLogsSuperseded` marks them at a practice reset. No business rules. |
| `phrase-practice-repository.js` | SQL for the card, due, introduction and status columns of `phrases`, and `phrase_review_logs` (session look-ups count only logs that still count; `markPhraseReviewLogsSuperseded` marks them at a practice reset). No business rules. |
| `frequency-repository.js` | SQL for `word_frequencies`: replaces or upserts the rank of each word form and looks up a rank by form, case-insensitively. No transactions; the service wraps them. |
| `practice-calendar.js` | Pure: calendar dates in the practice time zone (`Europe/Amsterdam`), start of a day and day arithmetic, DST-safe. Builds one `Intl` formatter per time zone and reuses it. |
| `fsrs-scheduling.js` | Pure wrapper around ts-fsrs: grades a card, serializes and parses cards, reads the scheduled interval, derives the learning status and the state name (from `CARD_STATE_VALUES`), builds the migration cards. |
| `priority-calculation.js` | Pure: parses the chapter from a section code, calculates a word's priority 1–3, compares queue order and computes a frequency rank for a Dutch text (apostrophes kept, numbers ignored, names in expressions unranked). |
| `new-word-allocation.js` | Pure: `calculateNewWordAllowance({ newPerDay, tierQuota, blockSize })` gives the tier quota of a block (each quota scaled by the block size and rounded down: 10 gives 7, 2, 1; 20 gives 14, 4, 2), and `allocateNewWords` chooses the New words of a block by priority quota, with unused slots going to P1, P2, P3. |
| `practice-set.js` | Pure: the practice set of a session, at most `new_per_day` (10) cards. `planSetRoom` puts the old cards first (no repeats, up to the set size) and returns the room left for new cards; `findOpenSet` keeps a set open while it was opened today and an item is still pending, with its `pendingIds` in pinned order; `openPracticeSet` pins items, `mapToStoredSet` and `mapFromStoredSet` convert to and from the saved `{ opened_on, opened_at, item_ids }`. |
| `migration-planning.js` | Pure: `planMigration` plans the one-time FSRS migration without writing (learning words spread over the next four days, most errors first; learned words due in 21 days; new words stay new) and `summarizeMigration` gives the counts the dry run and the real run both return. |
| `learning-deck.js` | Pure learning-deck rules (the deck is the current session's words): `applyAnswerToDeck` moves a word for one answer (a word enters on its first answer ever, or on a lapse, which it remembers as `isLapse`; good adds a right answer, again or hard add one required right answer up to 6 and keep the right answers given; the right answers reaching the required count means learned), `countTimesLeft`, `readDeckPosition` (from the `deck_*` columns), `describeDeckPosition` (the `deck` fields of the adapters) and `replayDeckAnswers` (for the rebuild). |
| `word-set-planning.js` | Factory `createWordSetPlanner({ practiceRepository })`: `planWordSet` returns the open set's words not finished yet (New, in the learning deck, or still due today), or plans the next set: the deck's words, then reviews due today (most overdue first, the ones graded in this session left out), then New words by priority quota (7, 2, 1 scaled to the room left). `countNewWordsOutside` counts the New words waiting outside the set, per priority. Reads only; writes nothing. |
| `practice-settings.js` | Service: reads the practice settings in camelCase (`readSettings`: `newPerDay` is the set size for words and phrases; the pinned sets `wordSet` / `phraseSet` as `{ openedOn, openedAt, itemIds }`, null when none is stored); `readActiveSettings` is the one guard that refuses practice before the FSRS migration. Saves the current chapter and the sets, tells whether FSRS is active and records the migration time. |
| `priority-service.js` | Service: recalculates priorities (`recalculatePriorities` publishes; `recalculatePrioritiesInTransaction` is for a caller that already holds a transaction and publishes nothing), ranks words from the frequency list (`assignNewWordRanking` also runs inside the caller's transaction), sets manual priorities and the current chapter, previews priorities for a chapter, and imports the frequency list in one transaction. Publishes `words` changes. |
| `practice-service.js` | Service: builds the practice batch (the session's word set: at most 10 words, old ones first, new ones in the room left; `counts.set_size`, `set_words`, `set_new_words`, `is_set_open`), pins the planned set when it is shown, grades a word (a New word outside the open set is refused; a wrong grade is recorded through `wordService.recordPracticeError`; the graded word carries its `error_count`), summarises the pinned set (`getRoundSummary`, the answers since the set opened), lists the struggling words (`listStrugglingWords`, read only), runs the migration to FSRS (dry run or real; one transaction, one `words` change after the commit) and calculates the practice statistics, with the phrases' own numbers under `phrases`. Publishes `words` changes. |
| `practice-reset-service.js` | Service: `resetPractice({ isDryRun })` starts the practice over: every word and phrase with a card, due date or introduction goes back to New, both practice sets are cleared and every review log that still counts is superseded, all in one transaction (the scheduled rows are read inside it); review logs and word errors are kept. Refused before the FSRS migration. Publishes `words` and `phrases` changes after a real reset. |
| `phrase-practice-service.js` | Service: the phrase batch (the session's phrase set: at most 10 phrases, phrases due today first, most overdue first, then the oldest New phrases in the room left; a pinned set stays until each phrase is graded that day), pinning a planned set when it is shown, grading a phrase once per session (a New phrase outside the open set is refused), and the phrase statistics. Gated by the same `readActiveSettings` guard as words. Publishes `phrases` changes. |
| `practice-tools.js` | MCP adapter: registers the twelve practice tools (`get_practice_batch`, `grade_word`, `get_phrase_batch`, `grade_phrase`, `set_current_chapter`, `set_word_priority`, `import_frequency_list`, `preview_priorities`, `migrate_to_fsrs`, `reset_practice`, `practice_stats`, `get_struggling_words`). `set_word_priority` returns the compact word of the word tools, with `priority_override`; `get_struggling_words` is read only. |
| `round-summary.js` | Pure: the round's words with answers, mistakes and difficulty, the hardest word, and the time spent without pauses. |
| `practice-routes.js` | REST adapter: `GET /api/practice/batch`, `POST /api/practice/grades`, `GET /api/practice/phrases/batch`, `POST /api/practice/phrases/grades`, `GET /api/practice/stats`, `GET /api/practice/struggling-words` (read only), `GET /api/practice/round-summary` (read only) and `GET /api/practice/priorities/preview`. |

## Conventions

- Repositories and the frequency repository are created with `create…Repository({ database })` and prepare their statements once in the factory.
- Values are bound parameters. JSON columns (`fsrs_card`, `review_logs.card_before`, `card_after`, settings) are stored as text; the settings repository parses and stringifies them.
- Pure modules take `now` and the other values as parameters and never read the clock or the database.
- Every practice service reads its settings through `practiceSettings.readActiveSettings()`, so the refusal before the migration reads the same everywhere.
- The rules are in `project-quality-standards` and in `docs/FSRS_PRACTICE_ARCHITECTURE.md` (sections 1 to 4, 11 for the reset, 13.1 for the struggling words and 18 for the practice sets, which replace the backlog gate of 10.1, the intake blocks of 12 and 13.2 and the extra new words).
