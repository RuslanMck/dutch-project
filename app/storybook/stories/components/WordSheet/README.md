The bottom sheet that opens when a word in Слова is tapped: the word with its article, translation, example on `garden-sand`, and detail tiles (status, next review, errors, chapter, topic) from `buildWordDetailView`. «Закрити» or a tap outside closes it. Read-only; delete stays in the table row.

Accessibility: a native `dialog` labelled by the word; Escape closes it.

Markup from `public/index.html`; styled by `storybook/styles/garden.css`.
