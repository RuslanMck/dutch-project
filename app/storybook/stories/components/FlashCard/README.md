The tappable flash card (`garden-raised`, `garden-radius-lg`, min 220px tall) that flips on tap, with 52px action rows under it.

Consumer supplies the Dutch side (`card-nl`, article in `.art`), Ukrainian side (`card-uk`) and an optional example (`card-ex`). Phrase cards show three grades (Знову / Важко / Добре); word cards show Не знаю / Далі.

Markup from `public/index.html`; styled by `storybook/styles/garden.css`.
